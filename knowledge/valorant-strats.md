# Valorant strats and setups (static game knowledge; the meta shifts with patches, so check current notes)

## How to learn lineups
KEYS: lineup, lineups, line up, line ups, line-up, one way, one ways, one-way, recon lineup, shock lineup, molly lineup, post plant lineup, dart
- Agents that live on lineups: Sova (recon bolt to clear a site, shock darts to stop a plant or defuse), Viper (molly, wall and orb for post-plant), Brimstone (molly post-plant), KAY/O (knife to clear or suppress), Killjoy (nanoswarm and Lockdown spots), Fade (Prowler/Haunt throws), Gekko (Mosh Pit). Omen/Astra/Brimstone/Clove smokes don't need lineups (they're aimed on the map).
- Learn 2 per site on your map pool first: one to clear/check (recon, drone path) and one to deny the plant or defuse (molly, shock). 20 lineups you half-know are worse than 4 you hit every time.
- Exact lineups need a picture or video (where to stand, where to aim). Look them up and give the link; describe roughly where they stand and what it's for. Never make up exact aim points.
- Practice them in a Custom Game with infinite abilities before using them in ranked.

## Agent setups and smoke spots
KEYS: killjoy, kj, cypher, deadlock, vyse, chamber, sentinel setup, setup, setups, trip, trips, tripwire, cage, cam, turret, alarmbot, nanoswarm, smoke spots, where to smoke, smokes, viper wall, viper setup
- Sentinel on defense: util covers the choke into your site (trip/alarmbot on the entrance, nanoswarm on the default plant, turret seeing the flank), and you play an off-angle that's covered by it. On attack: set up the flank behind your team.
- Change the setup every few rounds: a setup they've seen three times gets destroyed with util before they walk in.
- Trips at the choke exit where util can't break them from outside; cameras in spots they don't clear.
- Controllers smoke the sightlines defenders use to see the site (heaven, CT, back site), not the middle of the site. Smokes go down right before the entry swings, not 20 s early. Keep one smoke for the post-plant or retake.
- Viper: wall that cuts the site in half on attack (entry and plant behind it), orb on the main defender angle, molly lineups on the default plant for the post-plant.

## Retake and post-plant plans
KEYS: retake, retaking, post plant, post-plant, postplant, planted, spike down, defuse, afterplant, plant spot, default plant
- Post-plant: after the plant everyone moves OFF the spike to crossfires that see it (one close, one far, one watching flank). Deny the defuse from safety with molly/shock/Viper lineups. Don't all peek at once; the timer is on your side.
- Retake: group 3+, smoke their post-plant spots, flash/stun, clear close corners first, then the far ones, trade every duel. Tap the spike to bait a swing only when you know how many are left.
- Man advantage: retake fast and together. Man down with no time: save the gun.

## Ascent strats
KEYS: ascent
- Mid control decides Ascent: take Mid Courtyard with 2 players and util, then split A through Catwalk or B through Market.
- Attack A: info on A Main first (recon/drone), smoke Heaven and the Tree/CT side, flash over the Main wall, entry clears Generator and the close corners, trade fast. Plant so you can hold it from Main or Tree.
- Attack B: B Main is short and narrow. Smoke Market (the window from mid) and the back of site, flash Main, clear Boathouse and Lane quickly; a player in mid (Market/Pizza) stops the rotation.
- Defense: 2 A, 1 mid (Market or Catwalk), 2 B is standard; one early mid play for info with a trade. The A and Market doors can be closed to slow a rush or cut a rotation; breaking one is loud.
- Retakes come from the side the attackers didn't enter (A from Heaven/CT and Tree; B through Market and CT).

## Bind strats
KEYS: bind
- No mid. Two one-way teleporters connect the sides; they're loud, which makes fakes strong (show util on one site, teleport and hit the other).
- Hookah control = B control, Showers (A Short) control = A control: win those rooms early with util.
- Attack A: Showers + A Main/Lobby split, smoke Heaven/Tower, flash the Showers exit. Attack B: B Long + Hookah split, smoke Elbow and back site, clear Hookah first; Garden is the lurk path.
- Defense: one player each at Hookah and Showers for info; sites are close, so rotations are fast: don't over-stack early.

## Haven strats
KEYS: haven
- Three sites, so defenders are thin (1-2 per site). Take info early and hit the lightest site; fakes work well.
- Garage (mid → C) is key: it lets attackers split C (C Long + Garage) or pressure B.
- Attack A: A Long + A Short (Sewer) split, smoke Heaven and CT. Attack C: C Long + Garage/Cubby, smoke C Link/CT and the end of Long.
- Defense: sentinel anchors A or C, controller central to smoke for both, rotate only on real info.

## Split strats
KEYS: split
- Heavens win sites: smoke or flash them on attack, fight for them on defense. Ropes connect ramps and heaven.
- Mid (Vent, Mail, Mid Top) links both sites: taking it lets attackers split A (Ramps + Main) or B (Main + Mid/Link).
- Attack A: smoke Heaven and Screens, flash Ramps and Main. Attack B: smoke B Heaven and back site, clear Alley and Tower.
- Defense: contest the chokes early (Main, Ramps), then fall back toward heaven.

## Lotus strats
KEYS: lotus
- Three sites. Rotating doors (loud) and a breakable wall change the rotations: listen for them and punish.
- Attack A: A Main + Rubble, smoke Tree/Hut/Stairs, flash Rubble, clear Root and Drop. Attack C: C Main + Mound, smoke Hall and Bend/Waterfall, clear Mound.
- B is the smallest site and often lightly held: good for fast hits when defenders stack A or C.
- Defense: watch the door timings; one player can hold B and rotate through the doors.

## Icebox strats
KEYS: icebox
- B is huge and vertical: smoke Kitchen and Snowpile, plant near Yellow for a strong post-plant.
- A is about Belt and Nest duels; Pipes, Rafters and Screens give off-angles; the zipline gets you up.
- Mid (Boiler, Blue, Tube) gives splits to both sites.
- Defense: Op from Kitchen (B) or Nest (A); walls on Yellow or Belt stall rushes.

## Breeze strats
KEYS: breeze
- Huge and open with long sightlines: Op and Vandal, long smokes, Viper's wall is excellent.
- Attack A: A Hall/Shop + Cave, smoke Bridge and the back, plant on the Pyramids side. Attack B: B Main + Tunnel; B Window is a big angle, smoke or flash it.
- Mid (Wood Doors, Nest, Pillar) splits both sites.
- Defense: play long angles with a trade partner; never dry-peek a long lane.

## Pearl strats
KEYS: pearl
- No gimmicks. B Long is a big Op lane: smoke it, flash it, or fight it with a trade.
- Mid (Plaza, Shops, Doors, Connector) opens both sites (Link to A, Connector/Link to B).
- Attack A: A Main + Link, smoke Art/Dugout and Secret. Attack B: B Long + Hall/Link, smoke Tower and back site.
- Defense: 1-2 mid to stop splits; rotate through Link.

## Sunset strats
KEYS: sunset
- Three lanes: A Main, Mid (Courtyard/Top Mid) and B Main/Market. Mid control splits A (via Elbow/Link) and B (via Market).
- Attack A: smoke Elbow/Link and the back of site, flash Main. Attack B: clear Market, smoke Boba and back site, flash Main.
- Defense: 2-1-2, with the mid player deciding rotations.

## Fracture strats
KEYS: fracture
- Attackers spawn in the middle and can hit each site from two sides; defenders spawn between the sites.
- Split every hit (A Main + A Drop/Rope, B Arcade + B Main/Tree): defenders holding one side get sandwiched.
- Defense: one aggressive info player per side, then play retakes with util.

## Abyss strats
KEYS: abyss
- No walls at the edges: you can fall off, so knockbacks (Breach, Raze, Gekko) can kill. Don't fight near the edges.
- Plays like a two-site map with vertical angles: win the high ground. For exact spots use the live callouts, and look up current strats instead of guessing.
