# Valorant knowledge (static game knowledge; exact numbers can change with patches)

## Valorant coaching basics
KEYS: *valorant
STATIC GAME KNOWLEDGE. Prices, damage and ability details get tuned in patches: if the user says something changed, trust them; if you're unsure of an exact current number, say so instead of guessing. If an agent, ability or map isn't in your notes, say you're not sure instead of making it up.
- Rounds: 100 s. Spike blows 45 s after plant. Defuse 7 s; half (3.5 s) is saved if you let go. First to 13; at 12-12 overtime, win by 2.
- Agents (if your notes have a LIVE agent list, it's newer and wins over this one): Jett, Reyna, Raze, Phoenix, Yoru, Neon, Iso, Waylay (duelists); Sova, Breach, Skye, KAY/O, Fade, Gekko, Tejo (initiators); Brimstone, Omen, Viper, Astra, Harbor, Clove (controllers); Sage, Cypher, Killjoy, Chamber, Deadlock, Vyse, Veto (sentinels). A name in neither list isn't a Valorant agent (Zenyatta, Mercy etc. are Overwatch).
- The only guns: Classic, Shorty, Frenzy, Ghost, Sheriff, Stinger, Spectre, Bucky, Judge, Bulldog, Guardian, Phantom, Vandal, Marshal, Outlaw, Operator, Ares, Odin. No M4, AK, M416, P90, AWP (those are other games).
- Roles: Duelist makes space and entries. Initiator gets info and flashes/stuns for the entry. Controller smokes sightlines (wins or loses executes). Sentinel holds a site or flank with traps/walls.
- Pro habits: trade your teammate (stay close enough to kill his killer). Info before commitment. Don't re-peek the angle you just got a kill from. Use util before you swing, not after you die. Play for the round, not the kill.
- Mechanics: stop before you shoot (counter-strafe). Crosshair at head height, pre-aimed where they'll appear. Tap/burst at range, spray close. Getting shot slows you (tagging). Shift-walk is silent; running, reloading and some util are loud.

## Economy and buying
KEYS: eco, economy, credits, cred, money, buy, save, force, bonus, anti-eco, full buy, half buy, loss bonus, price, cost, shield, armor, armour, spectre, vandal, phantom, operator, op, sheriff, marshal, outlaw, judge, bulldog, guardian, stinger, ghost, frenzy, shorty, bucky, ares, odin, classic, 800, pistol round
STATIC (prices as of 2025; can change):
- Everyone starts 800 on pistol rounds (round 1 and 13). Win = 3000. Loss = 1900, then 2400, then 2900 on a loss streak. Kill = 200. Planting = 300 for each attacker. Max 9000.
- Prices: Classic free, Shorty 150, Frenzy 450, Ghost 500, Sheriff 800, Stinger 1100, Spectre 1600, Bucky 850, Judge 1850, Bulldog 2050, Guardian 2250, Phantom 2900, Vandal 2900, Marshal 950, Outlaw 2400, Operator 4700, Ares 1600, Odin 3200. Light shields 400, heavy 1000 (shield options have changed in recent patches: check if it matters).
- Full buy ≈ 3900+ (rifle + heavy + util). Team buys together: if most can't full buy, everyone saves (or everyone forces) — no lone half-buys.
- After winning pistol: enemy usually ecos. Buy Spectre/Stinger or Bulldog + armor, keep distance, don't give them free guns (anti-eco).
- Lost pistol: usually save round 2 (or a coordinated force with Sheriffs/Spectres if it breaks their economy), full buy round 3.
- Bonus round (after winning 2): play the guns you have; don't throw away a full buy next round for small upgrades.
- Rule of thumb for the next round: if your credits after this buy would drop below what you need to full buy next round AND your team isn't forcing, save.
- Low on money but a gun on the floor: drop/request from a teammate with 5k+ (they buy for you).

## Guns
KEYS: vandal, phantom, operator, op, sheriff, spectre, odin, judge, marshal, outlaw, guardian, bulldog, gun, weapon, rifle, spray, recoil, one tap
STATIC:
- Vandal: one-shot headshot kill at any range (with armor). Tap/burst at range. Best for holding long angles.
- Phantom: faster fire, easier spray, silenced, no tracers; headshot damage falls off at long range. Better for close/medium and spraying through smokes.
- Operator: one-shot body kill (except legs). Slow to scope and move; hold an angle and reposition after a shot.
- Sheriff: headshot kill up close to mid range; great eco gun if you can aim.
- Spectre: good eco/anti-eco SMG, fast running accuracy up close.
- Judge: shotgun, kills in 1-2 shots close; great on retakes/tight sites and holding short corners.
- Odin/Ares: wall-bang and spam through smokes/thin walls; good for denying defuses and holding chokes.

## Team comps and synergies
KEYS: comp, composition, team comp, synergy, synergies, duo, lineup with, pair, pick, who should i play, what agent, which agent, counter
STATIC:
- Standard comp: 1-2 duelists, 1-2 initiators, 1 controller (2 on big maps), 1 sentinel.
- Good duos: flash + entry (Breach/Skye/KAY/O + Jett/Raze/Neon), recon + shock (Sova solo), Viper/Brimstone post-plant molly lineups + Op holder, Killjoy/Cypher flank watch lets 4 play a site.
- Solo queue: pick what the team lacks (usually a controller or initiator). Always having a smoker matters more than a 3rd duelist.
- Duo queue: pair a flash/stun with the entry, or a Sova/Fade info + duelist who swings off the info immediately.
- Counters: vs rush-heavy teams play Sage/Killjoy/Deadlock and stack; vs Op use smokes to cut the angle and flash before contact; vs heavy info (Sova/Fade) spread out and dodge the reveal.

## Attack strategy
KEYS: attack, attacking, atk, t side, execute, exec, default, hit, rush, split, fake, lurk, lurker, map control, entry, plant, take site, go a, go b, go c
STATIC:
- Default: spread for map control and info (a lurker on one side, 3-4 taking space), save most util, decide the hit with ~40-50 s left based on info.
- Execute order: smokes on defender sightlines (heaven, CT, back site) → flashes/stuns on common angles → entry swings right behind the flash → trade entry within a second → plant in a spot you can defend from outside (off-angles, crossfire).
- Split hit: part through main, part through link/mid at the same time so defenders face two angles.
- Fake: loud util on one site, then rotate fast to the other; works when defenders over-rotate on sound.
- Lurk: only valuable if timed with the hit — catch rotators or flankers, don't die alone early.
- Man advantage (5v4): hit fast before they rotate help. Man down: slow default, play for picks, stack for a strong execute.

## Defense strategy
KEYS: defense, defence, defending, def, ct, ct side, hold, holding, anchor, rotate, rotation, retake, stack, stacking, push, aggro, aggressive, stall, delay, gatekeep
STATIC:
- Setup: usually 2-1-2 or 2-2-1 on 3 sites: sentinel anchors a site, controller mid-ish to smoke both, initiator gets early info.
- Don't all push; one aggressive info play with a trade is enough. After contact, fall back and use util to delay.
- Rotate on real info (seeing 3+, util usage, spike seen), not just one sound. Leave one player to hold the flank/lurk path.
- Stall: molly/slow/wall the choke, play off-angle behind util, give up space instead of dying.
- If site is lost and they have numbers, don't feed into post-plant: group up for a retake together.

## Retakes and post-plant
KEYS: retake, retaking, post plant, post-plant, postplant, planted, spike down, defuse, defusing, plant, planting, spike, lineup, molly lineup, deny, clutch, 1v1, 1v2, 1v3, 2v1, 2v2, 2v3, 3v2, last alive, last guy, tap, stick, fake defuse, ninja
STATIC:
- Post-plant: don't sit on spike. Play off-angles/crossfires that see the spike, or hide and use molly/Odin/Sova shock/Viper lineups to deny the defuse. Time is on your side: make them come to you.
- Retake: group up, use util together (flash/stun/smoke), clear close corners first, take fights 2-for-1 so you trade. Clear before touching the spike unless time forces it.
- Timing: spike has 45 s after plant, defuse 7 s. If a 1vX is impossible and you can't defuse in time, save your gun.
- Fake defuse (tap) to bait swings when the last enemy is hiding; half-defuse (3.5 s) sticks if you let go.
- Clutch: isolate 1v1s, use sound, don't swing two people at once, reposition after each kill, play time.

## Live match coaching style
KEYS: *live
When the user is mid-match giving quick info, answer like an in-game shotcaller:
- 1 to 3 short imperative callouts, max ~20 words total. No greetings, no emojis, no explanations unless asked.
- Use the map's real callouts and the match state (map, side, agent, money, spike, alive count, enemy positions).
- Examples of the style: "Hold tree. Don't swing yet. Flash off contact." / "Save here. Rifles next round." / "Play crossfire with Jett. Don't give the 1v1." / "Smoke heaven, flash site, then explode."
- Never pad with generic stuff like "stay calm", "focus", "communicate".

## Tilt, flame and mental
KEYS: tilt, tilted, tilting, flame, flaming, flamed, toxic, rage, raging, mad, angry, frustrated, annoyed, losing streak, lose streak, hardstuck, give up, quit, uninstall, bad day, throwing, thrower, blame, blaming, mental, calm down
- Be a friend first: one or two real lines, no strat dump. Take their side a bit, then help them reset.
- Mute or /mute the flamer (typing "/mute name" or the scoreboard mute), it costs nothing and they play better right after.
- Reset: breathe, drink water, only think about the next round. One round at a time, not the score.
- Two losses in a row while tilted: take a 10-minute break or play unrated/deathmatch, then come back.
- Don't argue in comms: one calm call ("I'll play with you B") does more than defending yourself.
- If it's them throwing, keep it honest but kind: say the one thing to fix, later, when they're calm.

## Training and practice
KEYS: practice, train, training, drill, routine, improve, get better, aim, aimlab, range, deathmatch, dm, warm up, quiz, test me, scenario, review, vod, mistake, why did i lose, what went wrong, coach, coach me, coaching
STATIC:
- "Coach me": if you already know their agent, rank or what they struggle with (from memory or this chat), start helping with that right away. Otherwise ask about ONE thing you haven't asked yet in this chat. Never ask the same question twice: if they skipped it, give them something useful anyway (a quick tip for their agent, a drill, a question about their last game). If they're in a match right now, switch to short live callouts.
- If they list agents ("Yoru, Viper"), that's probably their team comp or who they play: ask the map/side, or give each agent's job in one line.
- Warm-up (15-20 min): 5 min range bots/strafe bots at head height, 1-2 deathmatches focusing on one thing (counter-strafe or crosshair placement), 1 swiftplay/TDM for movement.
- Per-agent routine: learn 2-3 util setups per site for your map pool before learning 20.
- Map training: walk the map in custom game and learn the common angles for each site and the rotate times.
- Reviewing a round: find the one decision that lost it (dry peek, no trade, late rotate, wrong buy), say what to do instead, and why.
- Quizzes: ask one question at a time (callout, eco decision, clutch scenario, positioning), wait for the answer, then grade it briefly and explain.
- Keep plans short: a few bullet points, not essays.

## Match review checklist
KEYS: *review, review, scoreboard, stats, what went wrong, why did we lose, why did i lose, acs, adr, kast, k/d, kda, first blood, first death, econ
How a coach reads a game (use what's visible; say what's missing):
- Score and side: which half went wrong? Losing most attack rounds = executes/entries; most defense rounds = setups/rotations/retakes.
- First deaths vs first bloods: dying first a lot (especially not as the duelist) is the #1 round-loser: dry peeks, no util, no trade partner. Fix: peek with util or a teammate, hold the angle instead of taking it.
- K/D with low ACS/ADR: kills came late or in lost rounds (exit frags); high ADR low kills: damage without finishing (tagging, no re-peek plan, trade missing).
- Role check: duelists should get first contact with a trade behind them; initiators get info/flash before the entry; controllers get smokes down on time (lower ADR is fine); sentinels hold flank and survive (low deaths, high KAST).
- Econ: many losses after winning pistol = bad anti-eco (fighting close, giving guns); lost bonus rounds; forcing alone.
- Plants/defuses and clutches: few plants on attack = hits too slow or no entry; lost post-plants = everyone sat on spike, no crossfire.
- Always end with ONE focus for the next game (e.g. "don't take first contact without util") and one drill (e.g. 10 min Range strafe bots at head height, then 1 deathmatch only counter-strafing).
